BOFU Statics Swipe File v3

46 bottom-of-funnel static ads in 10 BOFU types. 29 new from a Nov 22, 2026 Foreplay pull (water treatment dealers, home-services lead gen, retargeting), 17 kept from v1/v2 after re-screening for BOFU only. 46 still running at pull; median 364 days running; all ran 30+ days.

BOFU definition: the ad assumes the viewer already wants the category and pushes the decision: direct offers (free in-home test, quote, consult, inspection; financing; bundle; guarantee), comparison vs a competitor or alternative, 'getting quotes?' / second opinion, reviews as proof to choose, 'why us' checklists, objection-crushing (price, install, trust), retargeting-style reminders, urgency or limited slots, and local 'homeowners in X' book-now. Excluded: awareness, education, lifestyle, brand-story and problem-discovery ads.
Rules for every rip: never show or say a price. Offer line: $0 down, 0% interest, payments as low as $69/mo. Proof line: 1000+ customers, 5 stars. Warranty: extended 15-year warranty (never 'lifetime'; the website still says 10 years). Lead offer: free in-home water test. Miami-Dade & Broward. Reviews: real only, else [real review needed]. EWG facts (from Doug's US vs THEM sheet): 18 contaminants above EWG health guidelines; PFHxS 2,575x EWG's guideline; arsenic 125x. Always add the fine print: *EWG Tap Water Database, Miami Dade Water and Sewer Authority (PWS FL4130871), checked Nov 22, 2026. EWG health guidelines are stricter than federal limits; Miami's water meets federal limits. Your home may differ. Never say the water is illegal or unsafe.
