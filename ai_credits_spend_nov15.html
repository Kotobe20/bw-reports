1. Daily cap. Do not spend more than $40 of Viktor credits in a day unless Doug or AG says yes in Slack first. If you are about to cross the cap, stop and post a one-line warning in #admin with what’s left to do.
2. Cheap model first. Use the cheapest model that can do the job for Pulse recaps, job flags, list cleanups, and short answers. Save the big model for money decisions, legal wording, or when someone asks you to think harder.
3. One Pulse, not ten. Run the Daily Financial Pulse once each morning. Do not rebuild the full report when one number changes. Patch that line only. Batch similar asks into one run.
4. Don’t re-read the world. Do not re-pull all of Mercury, Drive, Slack, Close, and Gmail on every question. Reuse the last good pull if it is under 12 hours old, and say the as-of time. Only refresh a source when the question needs new data.
5. Ask before heavy jobs. Before any job that will transcribe long Looms, read hundreds of calls, crawl many docs, or likely cost over $20 in credits, post the plan and a cost guess in #admin and wait for a yes.
6. Report your own use. At the end of each Pulse, add one line: Viktor credits used today, remaining balance, and Anthropic card charges in the last 24 hours (count and dollars) from Mercury. If Anthropic has more than 3 reloads in a day, ping Doug and AG the same day.

8. No silent $1,000 reloads. If a Viktor auto top-up over $100 is about to fire, warn #admin first. Prefer a $100 top-up over a $1,000 top-up unless someone asked for the larger one.
