2. The strategy (why this machine exists)

  • Speed to lead. Hormozi and ACQ: speed, personalization, and volume drive show rates. ACQ's plan is a setter call within 60 seconds, a double dial, a text, then this follow-up. Only 32.8% of new leads get a real conversation in week one, and 96% of sold jobs come from leads that do. This sequence is the written half of that first hour, not a replacement for the dial.
  • Pre-frame before the call and before the visit. ACQ told us to build a founder VSL that pre-sells the premium buyer before the setter calls: hardness vs PFAS vs metals, why a generic two-tank system is not the same, what happens next. Doug's 5 minute 33 second pre-sales VSL is that asset. Steven's memo: if they watch it, show rates go up, and conversion goes up once a rep is pitching. The first text and email exist to get the watch, from Doug, with a reason to click, before the specialist calls.
  • Show rate is the expensive number. Vincent / ACQ: in person closes harder than phone, and the in-home specialist's calendar cannot be wasted. "No one gets booked who isn't ready for that conversation." Once they book, the job of the machine changes: confirm both decision makers, remind at 24h / 12h / 3h, confirm by phone the night before and the morning of, and get the VSL in front of people who booked on Calendly and skipped the unbooked lane (Chad: "we need to fix this").
  • Cadence. ACQ's nurture rule of thumb is 3 texts on day 1, 2 on day 2, and 1 after that. The live English sequence is close: first SMS, first email, and a 3-hour SMS on day 0; one SMS on day 1; one SMS on day 2; then email on day 3, SMS on day 4, SMS on day 7. I kept those delays on purpose so we are not inventing a new clock. I added VSL reminder lines into existing touches rather than stacking more messages.
  • Copy standard. Halbert and Ogilvy: flag the South Florida homeowner, one clear promise, a reason to click, specific pictures (shower glass, fridge filter), a hard close, no invented proof or deadlines. Claims stay inside Doug's VSL. I did not strip any live Amanda lines. The Day 3 email's "what it costs" line is already live; it stays until you cut it.

5. Stop rules, and the watched-vs-not branch

Live stop rules. Before every later unbooked message, Close checks the lead again and pauses the sequence if any of these are true: they text us (any inbound text); a call lasts more than 2 minutes either way; a meeting is booked; a Sold, Not Sold, Send Agreement, or Water Consult Completed card is logged; status leaves Potential; they get a Housecall Pro customer ID. A 15-minute guard during 8 AM to 8 PM ET pauses duplicates (same phone, or enrolled in both languages).

What that means. The moment they book, they leave this lane. That is correct for stopping "are you free for a 10-min call" texts after they already have an in-home. It is also why Calendly bookers can miss Doug's video. The booked lane in the next section is the fix.

Watched vs not. A plain unlisted YouTube link is not trackable per lead today. Until we have a unique link or a simple page that writes a "watched VSL" event into Close, everyone in the unbooked lane gets the short reminder lines. Those lines are harmless if they already watched. Once tracking exists: skip the "if you missed it" lines for watchers, and lean the next touch on booking. I would not delay launching the first SMS and email on this.
