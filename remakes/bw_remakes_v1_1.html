South Florida homeowner (20s to 40s, often a couple or roommates) who won't drink the tap water, buys cases of bottled water every week and relies on a fridge filter. Seen in AD 01 and AD 03 (two-friends kitchen skits).
Pains
  • Carrying 'stupid cases of bottled water' every week
  • Tap water tastes and smells like chemicals (chlorine/ammonia smell)
  • Thinks the fridge filter is enough, but it only covers one tap
Desires
  • Drink straight from the kitchen faucet
  • Stop buying and hauling bottled water
  • Filtered water at every tap, including the shower
Objections
  • 'I've already got a filter in my fridge. What's the difference?'
  • 'That thing just looks expensive.'
  • 'Who's installing this thing?'
Their words
  • “Nobody drinks the tap water here”
  • “Bro, that's disgusting”
  • “stupid cases of bottled water”
  • “What kind of sorcery is this?”
  • “filtering my water with a chapstick”
  • “every tap in my house”
