2. Key Takeaways from Chad's 4 Videos

Video 1: Tyler Link × Alex Hormozi (10% → 40% Concrete Consult)

Core 3× Lever youtu.be/HFPrWE8JAIU

  • Never email a quote and hope: Tyler quoted rough price on the phone, visited, then emailed quotes and closed 10%. Closing in the room delivers 3× the close rate. Biscayne's version is hanging up and texting a deposit link or PandaDoc.
  • Set call rules: Qualify, give only a wide range ($3,500–$9,000+), and state out loud that the Zoom is to design the system and collect the deposit. Require all decision makers on the Zoom.
  • Order of operations: Fix the sales motion first (1 week of training + closing on the call), then pricing. A 10% price raise doubles profit on a 10% net business, and 3×–4× close rate × margin = 8× profit.

Video 2: Hormozi with Caleb, Keys Roofing ($10M Service Playbook)

VSL + Menu Close youtu.be/o64cI6tebnU

  • Two short VSLs, not one long one: (1) A ~7-minute pre-Zoom VSL covering local South Florida proof, old way vs new way, and belief-breaks (fridge filters, softeners, price). (2) A second short VSL built specifically for the missing spouse.
  • Menu prescription close: First unsell what they do not need to build trust, prescribe what fits, give a fake choice ("Floridian Pro or Lite?", "Tuesday or Thursday?"), and take the deposit on the phone.
  • Sell speed instead of discounts: Offer a priority install boost (waived if Biscayne misses the window) rather than cutting price.

Video 3: Phil Risher, Phlash Consulting ($100M Home Service Offers)

Value Equation youtu.be/I0mgS6dv29c

  • Crush effort and time delay: Affluent $3.5k–$9k buyers pay to remove hassle. Emphasize 1-visit install, HOA/permit handling, old unit removal, before/after water test photos, and zero babysitting.
  • Pricing guide + short video on SMS: Never send a naked quote. Send a pricing guide + short video in the text thread and follow up on that thread.
  • Two-path close & financing downsell: Offer system-only vs system + annual protection plan (filter changes, water tests, priority service, rollover credit). Use financing as the money-objection downsell, not a footnote.

Video 4: MoreMozi with Cody ($140M Virtual Roofing)

Sequencing & Risk youtu.be/1hcgUXiFYm8

  • Do not change 5 things at once: Proof that high-ticket virtual home service closes at scale. Fix sales execution and cut low-ask habits before rewriting the whole offer or ad account.
  • Specific guarantee formula: Use "If you don't X in Y time, we will Z." Never promise a reckless 100% refund when hardware costs are high. Guarantee water-test standards within 14 days, on-time install, and clean home, backed by a profit/fee slice or free fix.

4. Sequenced Execution Plan

Week 1: Sales Motion Only ($0 Spend, Biggest 3× Lever)

  1. Lock the Zoom exit rule: No Zoom that reaches price ends without 3 direct asks, 2 concrete install dates, and either a live deposit cleared on the phone or a booked clock-time callback.
  2. Enforce the both-spouses rule: Set call gives wide range ($3,500–$9,000+) and requires both decision makers on the Zoom. If one is missing, reschedule the close and text the 2-minute recap.
  3. Run the 3-tier menu close: Unsell what they do not need → prescribe Pro/Lite/Standard → "Option A or B?" → "Tuesday or Thursday?" → collect deposit while staying on the line.
  4. Handle money objections without discounting: Lead with financing (US Bank / Affirm) or priority-install speed instead of cutting ticket price.
  5. Stop Meta test bleed: Cap new Meta ad tests at $150 spend if <2 leads; shift budget back into proven Spanish statics and winning Doug whitelisting.

Weeks 2–3: Pre-Zoom VSLs & Risk-Reversal Guarantee

  1. Ship the 7-minute pre-Zoom VSL: Play on the quiz/Calendly confirmation page with South Florida homes, local water report proof, and belief-breaks.
  2. Ship the 2-minute Spouse VSL: Sent immediately when a partner could not join the call.
  3. Launch 1 clear guarantee: Written water-quality target within 14 days + on-time install promise (we fix free or waive priority fee/slice of profit if missed).
  4. Replace naked quote PDFs: Text a pricing guide + short video in the SMS thread and follow up there.

Weeks 3–6: Money Model & Lead Mix

  1. Two-path close: System-only vs system + annual protection plan (filter swaps, annual test, priority service, rollover credit).
  2. B2B referral engine: Realtors, luxury property managers, well drillers, and remodelers so Meta is not the only lead source.
