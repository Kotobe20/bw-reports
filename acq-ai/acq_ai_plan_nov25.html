Diagnosis

Your bottleneck is not lead volume. It is sales execution between lead arrival and payment:

  • Only 32.8% get a real conversation in week one.
  • Reps ask for the sale on only 20% to 30% of qualified calls.
  • You have a $100K selling technician whose calendar cannot be wasted.
  • You are break-even, so every build must improve conversion before adding spend.

Build the sales machine first. Then send better leads into it. Speed, personalization, and volume are the four drivers of show rates.

2Install the setter system

Owner: Steven and Ramani, days 1 to 3

Ramani should begin as a pure setter, not a closer. His job is speed and appointment quality, not explaining filtration.

Setter script:

“Hi, is this [Name]? This is Ramani with Biscayne Water. You requested information about whole-home water filtration for your home in [city]. I'm calling quickly because we respond while the request is fresh.

Before I schedule anything, are you the homeowner, and will everyone involved in the decision be available?”

Qualify:

  • Homeowner?
  • Single-family home?
  • City?
  • Main concern?
  • Timeline?
  • Both decision-makers attending?

Pre-frame:

“The appointment is with [Technician Name], who previously trained Culligan technicians throughout South Florida. He'll test your water, inspect the installation location, and build the exact recommendation for your home.

He is not coming just to leave a quote. He'll review the options with you and, if the system makes sense, help you get it installed. So if the solution fits, are you prepared to make a decision at that appointment?”

Then:

“I have [day/time] or [day/time]. Which works better?”
No “we'll send a quote.” No unqualified appointments.

3Build two VSLs, not five

VSL 1: Landing page VSL
Purpose: pre-sell the premium buyer before the setter calls. Explain the difference between hardness, PFAS, arsenic, and heavy metals. Show proof, process, warranty, and why pricing differs. Do not make medical promises. Use certified reduction claims only where documented. EPA recommends certified filters for PFAS reduction. (epa.gov)

VSL 2: In-home iPad VSL
Purpose: occupy the decision-maker while the technician builds the proposal. Founder explains why cheap systems are not equivalent, why the recommendation is home-specific, and what happens next: choose the system, choose payment, schedule installation.

Start with these two. Add FAQ videos only after the funnel is live and measured. Keep the landing page mobile-first and match ad language to page language.
