Doug's Halbert lens on #1

  • Hungry crowd: people who have heard of the product and don't trust it. That's our buyer too: "is a whole-house system a gimmick?"
  • Hook: it says the viewer's own doubt in their words. That's the A-pile feel, not an ad voice (BL Ch. 11, 18).
  • Proof: an on-camera demo with a number on a meter. Ogilvy's "honest demonstration" (HCATS #13–17).
  • Offer + risk reversal: a free gift, 90-day money back, and reviews in the caption.
  • CTA: one action: "click the link, try it for yourself."
  • Misses (we fix these): unproven health claims, "limited stock" urgency, and a 92s runtime that's 40s too long.
