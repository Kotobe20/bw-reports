2. What ACQ got right

  1. Don’t switch CRMs. Close already runs P1–P6, Sold cards, PandaDoc, HCP and Viktor. Agreed.
  2. Shared queues, not rep-owned leads. Same as our model today: any rep on shift dials any lead.
  3. Automate the low-touch stuff. Today every text and email is sent by hand, and 0 automated messages have gone out since 11/8 (Viktor).
  4. Front-load days 0–7 and double-dial. Only 29% of leads get a 2nd dial within 2 minutes today (Viktor).
  5. The Day 1 question text is good. “Spots and buildup, chlorine smell, or what the family drinks?” is easy to answer and tells the rep what to sell. Keep it.

3. Pushback, one by one

3.1 No Salesmsg or Twilio. Close already does this.

  • Fact: Close Workflows have SMS steps, email steps, delays from 1 minute to 365 days, a send window, and “goals” that pause the run when the lead texts back, calls in, emails back, books a meeting, or changes status. help.close.com/feature-guide/workflows · /workflows/workflow-steps
  • Fact: If a lead texts STOP, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE, Close blocks more texts to that number. help.close.com/feature-guide/sms-and-mms
  • Fact: Close registers A2P 10DLC itself (through Twilio) under Settings › Phone & Voicemail › US Compliance. help.close.com/feature-guide/sms-and-mms/a2p-10dlc
  • Why it matters: a second texting tool means a second inbox. Replies would live outside Close, Viktor’s stats would miss them, and the P-queue rule “last message was inbound” would stop working. You’d also pay for one more tool.
  • Assumption: we’re on a plan that has Workflows (Growth or Scale), because the old 31-step sequence ran in Close until 11/8. Check the Plan page. Workflows are Growth/Scale only: help.close.com/feature-guide/workflows
  • Assumption: our 10DLC is already approved, because reps send texts from Close today (1.3 per lead) and Close says US texts don’t deliver without approval. Check US Compliance to confirm.

3.2 “It’s [Rep] from Biscayne Water” doesn’t work with unassigned leads

  • Fact: A Close Workflow text sends from one user: the workflow owner, one named user, or a user saved in a lead field. That user must turn on “Allow automated Workflows to send on your behalf.” /workflows/workflow-steps
  • No one owns new leads, so every text would come from one person’s number. Replies would go to that person, and so would the spam risk.
  • Fix: sign texts as “the Biscayne Water team” and send them from one team texting number that the reps watch in the Close inbox. Don’t use a rep’s personal line. Assumption: whether a Workflow can send from a shared team number in our setup needs a test on a dummy lead.

3.3 “Just pulled your [City] water report” is a lie, unless we really pull one

  • At minute 2, nobody has pulled anything. The Slack version goes further: “Got your report done: [X] contaminants over guideline.” We would have to make up X for every city.
  • Halbert: a grabber that isn’t honest is “a cheap shot. People resent this kind of fraud” (BL Ch. 14). Ogilvy: never write an ad you wouldn’t want your family to read (HCATS #5).
  • Honest fix: every city posts a yearly water quality report. I can build a city-to-link list for Miami-Dade and Broward, and texts can offer to “walk you through” that real report. If we ever quote a contaminant count, it must come from a named source. EWG “health guidelines” are not legal limits, so say “above EWG’s health guideline,” not “over the limit.” Assumption: I have not built or checked that list yet.

3.4 “All-in price” by email breaks our rule

  • Our rule (Viktor’s write-up to ACQ): a text’s only job is to book the call. We never send price, menus or agreements by text. Our ad rules ban system prices, and the objection playbook says financing comes only after the system is picked.
  • The Slack version’s no-sale text, “we’ll still honor today’s pricing,” is fake urgency unless that price really ends. Our hard rule: no fake urgency.
  • Fix: Email D0 tells them what happens on the call. No price. The price is said on the call, and in writing only after someone attends and asks for it.

3.5 Too many texts, plus the legal basics

  • ACQ’s 0–7 plan comes to about 13 calls and 9 texts (Viktor’s count). Day 0 alone has 4 calls, 2 texts and 1 email.
  • Fact: Florida §501.616(6) says no commercial sales calls before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m., and no more than 3 calls in 24 hours on the same subject. Assumption: whether this covers texts, and whether we fall under it, is a lawyer question. leg.state.fl.us §501.616
  • Fact: Florida’s consumer agency lists 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. local time for calls and texts. fdacs.gov “What hours can a telemarketer call or text?”
  • Fact: Florida §501.059(8) requires prior express written consent for sales texts sent by an automated system. A checked box counts as a signature. leg.state.fl.us §501.059 Assumption: I have not checked if our Meta EN/ES forms and /quiz have a consent line that names Biscayne Water and texts.
  • Fact: Close’s 10DLC rules: name the sender in the first text, put “Reply STOP to opt out” in the first text of every Workflow, keep texts under 160 characters, and watch the opt-out rate. help.close.com/feature-guide/sms-and-mms/a2p-10dlc-stay-compliant
  • Fix: set the Workflow send window to Mon–Sat, 9 a.m.–7 p.m. Eastern. That is inside every rule above. Send no more than 2 texts on day 0 and 1 a day after that, and drop to 6 texts in 7 days. Spanish gets 4 texts, because Spanish leads already say we call too much.

3.6 Spanish leads need Spanish copy

  • ACQ’s iMessage copy is English only. We have 6 Spanish queues and Spanish Meta forms. Build a separate Spanish workflow, triggered by the language set by the ES zap (387452236).
  • Fact: accents like “¿” and “é” switch a text to a different encoding, so each part holds fewer characters. Spanish texts may bill as 2 parts. help.close.com/account-management/variable-usage-costs…

3.7 Stop rules ACQ didn’t spell out

The run must stop the moment any of these happen:

  1. The lead texts, calls or emails us (Close goals, on by default).
  2. A rep has a real conversation. Assumption: Close has no “outbound call connected” goal I could confirm. Workaround: start the workflow from the first call with no answer (Close supports “Call event” triggers), not from lead created. Then no one gets “can I call you now?” right after talking to us.
  3. A Sold or Not Sold card is logged, or a meeting is booked (lead status change goal, plus meeting booked).
  4. They text STOP (Close blocks it automatically).

3.8 No task steps, or the P-queues go empty

  • Viktor found that in our Close setup, any open task drops a lead out of every P-queue. ACQ’s plan adds “system tasks” and call tasks.
  • Fix: keep the P-queues as the dial engine. Workflows send texts and emails only, with no task steps.

3.9 No new “Meta → Close → Sequence” zap

  • Leads already enter Close through zaps 387452193 (EN), 387452236 (ES), 387452261 (Partnership) and 380204283 (website/quiz). A new zap would double up.
  • Close can start the workflow on its own, so we need 0 extra Zapier tasks. A Zapier version would use tasks on every send for every lead. At about 9 inbound leads a day (119 leads in 13 days), that adds up fast.
  • Don’t limit it to Meta. Google, /quiz and website leads need follow-up too. Question: do Partnership leads get it?

3.10 Smaller things

  • 10DLC won’t fix “Spam Likely” on calls. 10DLC is for texts. Call labels are fixed by Close’s Trusted Calling step (STIR/SHAKEN and Voice Integrity), which is part of the same registration, and by not over-dialing one number. /a2p-10dlc
  • Photos: Close Workflow texts can’t send photos (no MMS). Put before/after photos in email, and only use real ones we have permission for. /workflows/workflow-steps
  • Blanks: [street], [their problem] and [time] are only filled if that field exists on the lead. If it doesn’t, the text reads “fix the .” Only use fields we always have, or fixed words.
  • “Install in [nearby city] with the same issue” is only OK if it’s true. That needs a real list of recent installs by city.
