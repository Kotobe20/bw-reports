TL;DR

  1. One phone catches everything. Every CallRail number forwards to one line, 786-823-xxxx. The call flow is: record, greeting, dial that line, then voicemail. No ring group, no business hours, no backup person. Who owns 786-823-xxxx is unknown.
  2. Our main number is a tracking number labeled "Google My Business." 305-846-7352 is on every website page, the quiz, the shop, the PPC page, and the listings we found. CallRail tags it as GMB. So website visitors, directory callers and Google profile callers all land in one bucket. That is why almost every call says GMB (28 of 36 last full week).
  3. Some calls never touch CallRail. Every QuickBooks estimate and invoice shows +1 (917) 625-4805 as our phone. That number is not in CallRail. A customer who calls it from a quote is not counted. Owner: unknown (public lookup says it is an AT&T mobile number in New York).
  4. The tracking setup has holes. The swap number for 846-7352 is 305-555-0144, which looks like a placeholder. The pool swap number 305-363-1473 is not on any page we found. The main website has no CallRail swap script at all. The Homestead and postcard numbers got 0 calls this week.
  5. Calls are light, and many are short. CallRail sees about 5.4 a day, not 10. Many answered calls are only 15 to 25 seconds, and 10 callers hung up before pickup (Nov 10 to 17). We cannot tell yet if the other calls Chad expects are happening somewhere CallRail can't see.

What we did NOT find

  • No sign that calls are forwarded to a stranger or a RevPilot number. Every CallRail number goes to 786-823-xxxx, and we do not know who owns it. So we cannot rule it out. This is the first thing to confirm.
  • Close phone settings: unknown. RevPilot still controls the Close login, and the Zapier Close connection has needed a reconnect since Nov 16.
