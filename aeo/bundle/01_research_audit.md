# Research + site audit (Nov 22, 2026)

## Method and limits

- I ran ~15 web searches through an AI answer engine (the search tool returns a synthesized AI answer plus its cited links), and fetched primary sources (WASD 2025 CCR PDF, Hollywood 2025 CCR PDF, EWG, Miami Herald).
- I could NOT log into ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini or Grok and run the prompts there. The citation patterns below come from the search engine's AI answers and the pages it cited, which overlap heavily with what those engines use but are not identical. The tracking list in 05_rollout_tracking.md is how to measure the real engines monthly.
- No search-volume data was available. Priorities are judgment (buyer intent x gap x existing assets), to be checked against Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

**Patterns**

1. Winners publish answer-first local pages with exact numbers from the utility report (Culligan, Florida Tap Report, Galene, flowwaterfl). AI quotes numbers.
2. AI leans on review counts. Competitors show 119 to 1,632 Google reviews; the place card for Biscayne Water shows 48. Review volume is the biggest off-site gap.
3. Third-party sources that get cited: EWG, utility CCRs, Miami Herald, WLRN, Axios, FIU, Angi, imovingtoflorida, Reddit, Nextdoor. Yelp/BBB did not surface for Biscayne Water at all.
4. Several competitors publish prices. Our rule says no system price, so cost questions must be answered with the financing line and "flat written quote after a free test." Expect to lose some pure "how much" prompts; win them on trust instead.
5. Spanish answers are thin and mostly from small competitors: a real opening.

## Site audit: biscaynewater.com (repo Kotobe20/biscaynewater @ origin/main 2dd7353, Nov 19)
**Already strong (do not rebuild):**

- Static HTML on Cloudflare Pages, generated by Python scripts (scripts/generate-*.py). Deploy via deploy.sh (wrangler 3). CLAUDE.md documents everything.
- robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Bingbot, Applebot, meta-externalagent, DuckAssistBot; Content-Signal search/ai-input/ai-train = yes.
- llms.txt + llms-full.txt, Markdown twins for every page (Accept: text/markdown), MCP server, A2A agent card, agent skills, OpenAPI. This is far ahead of every competitor.
- sitemap.xml: 100 URLs (80 under /locations/, 9 blog, 4 services).
- Homepage JSON-LD: LocalBusiness, Service x3, OfferCatalog, FAQPage (6 Q), WebSite. City pages: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage (5 Q), BreadcrumbList.
- 8 blog posts already target key questions (is Miami tap water safe, best company in South Florida, softener worth it, RO vs whole-home, cost).

**Gaps and conflicts:**

1. **Facts are out of date vs the new rules.** Site/llms.txt/BIZ dict say "100+ customers", "5.0 stars from 60+ Google reviews", "10-year tank and valve warranty" (owner set 10-year on Nov 19 per CLAUDE.md), "Founded 2009". New rules: "1000+ customers, 5 stars", "extended 15 year warranty". AI engines will see conflicting facts across pages; they must be one set, everywhere.
2. **Palm Beach.** Site claims Palm Beach County service (30 Palm Beach County pages: the county hub, Boca Raton and West Palm Beach nested trees (8 pages), and 21 flat city pages). The brief says the real area is Miami-Dade + Broward. Needs an owner decision.
3. **No utility data pages.** Nobody owns "what is in [city] water" with exact 2025 CCR numbers on a local installer site except Culligan/Florida Tap Report. Biggest content gap.
4. **No PFAS page** even though the company sells a certified PFAS Guard and Miami-Dade PFOS is 33 ppt vs a 4 ppt limit.
5. **No Spanish pages.** Miami-Dade is majority Hispanic.
6. **Unsourced claims in existing copy:** "hardness typically 200 to 350 ppm" (WASD does not publish hardness), "water purer than major bottled brands", "roughly 2 cents per gallon". Each needs a source or a rewrite.
7. **Duplicate city structure:** legacy flat /locations/{city}-fl/ (62) + nested /locations/{county}/{city}/ (Karan's Oct '26 tree). Two pages per city confuse AI and Google.
8. **No About/team page, no reviews page, no sources page** (E-E-A-T and citation targets).
9. AggregateRating: there is a 2-star Google review excluded from the site slider by owner choice; any AggregateRating markup must use the real Google numbers, all reviews included, or be omitted.

## Verified facts used in the copy (primary sources)

- WASD 2025 CCR (miamidade.gov PDF): Main System PFOS 33 ppt (7-33), PFOA 13 (3-13), PFHxS 7, PFNA 5; chloramine 2.5 ppm avg (ND-4.0); TTHM 50 ppb (9-59); HAA5 34 ppb (10-43); lead 90th pct 3.2 ppb, 1 of 102 homes over AL; fluoride addition stopped June 6, 2025, natural 0.1-0.3 ppm; South Dade PFOS 32, PFOA 13, nitrate 7 ppm. PFAS violations not reported until 2029.
- EWG Tap Water Database (2026 data set): Miami Dade Water and Sewer Authority serves 2,377,460; 18 contaminants exceed EWG health guidelines; in compliance with federal standards Jan-Mar 2026.
- Miami Herald Aug 7, 2026: FIU found 80-95% of Miami-Dade samples (about 72 samples, 18 ZIPs) over 4 ppt; hotspots Little Havana, Pembroke Pines, Homestead, Hialeah, Grapeland Heights. WASD says no filter is needed.
- FLERA conference deck Sept 2026: EPA May 18, 2026 proposed rule keeps PFOA/PFOS 4 ppt, allows extension to 2031, proposes rescinding PFHxS/PFNA/GenX/HI limits.
- Hollywood 2025 CCR: chloramine 2.42 ppm (0.03-3.2), lead 3.5 ppb; PFOS "0.02" and PFOA "0.006" (March 2024) with no unit printed in the table.
- Biscayne PFAS Guard: IAPMO R&T, NSF/ANSI 53, PFOA/PFOS/PFHxS/PFNA/PFHpA up to 99.82% (per repo build-customquote.py; the public listing URL is not in the repo).
