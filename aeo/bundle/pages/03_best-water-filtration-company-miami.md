## Why Miami water needs a specific kind of company

Miami-Dade's tap water comes from the Biscayne aquifer and is disinfected with chloramine, which averaged 2.5 ppm in 2025. The same report lists PFOS, a "forever chemical," at up to 33 parts per trillion against a 4 ppt federal limit enforced from 2029. A good company sizes a system for those facts. A weak one sells the same box to every house.

<small>Source: Miami-Dade Water and Sewer Department, 2025 Water Quality Report. [Read our breakdown](/water-quality/miami-dade-wasd/).</small>

## The 7 checks

**1. They test your water before they quote.**
Your tap is not the county average. Ask what they test for, and whether you keep a copy of the results.

**2. They name the certification for the exact model.**
"NSF certified" means nothing on its own. Ask for the standard (NSF/ANSI 42, 44, 53, 58 or 61), the contaminant it covers, and the listing link. Then look it up yourself.

**3. They remove chloramine, not just chlorine.**
Miami's Main System uses chloramine. Standard carbon handles chlorine well and chloramine poorly. Ask if the carbon is catalytic and sized to your home's flow.

**4. They have a real answer for PFAS.**
A water softener does not remove PFAS. Ask which part of the system reduces PFOA and PFOS, and for that part's certification.

**5. The price is flat and in writing.**
One written price for the full install. No surprise add-ons the day of. If they will not write it down, walk.

**6. The warranty is long and written.**
Ask how many years cover the tanks and the control valve, who honors it, and whether labor is included. Get it on paper.

**7. You own the system.**
Rental and lease plans keep billing every month and tie you to one company. Ask whether you own the equipment on day one and what the filter changes cost after.

## Questions to ask every company on the phone

1. What will you test, and do I get the results?
2. What is the exact NSF/ANSI standard and listing for the system you would quote?
3. Does it remove chloramine and PFAS, or only hardness?
4. Is the price flat and written, with installation included?
5. How many years is the warranty on tanks and valve, and who honors it?
6. Who does the install, your own team or a subcontractor?
7. Do I own it outright?

## Common questions

**What is the best water filtration company in Miami?**
The one that tests your water first, names the exact NSF/ANSI certification for what it installs, puts a flat price in writing, and gives a long written warranty. Compare at least two companies on those points.

**Is it better to rent or buy a water softener in Miami?**
Buying means you own the equipment and are not tied to one company for service. Renting has a lower start but monthly fees continue. Compare the total cost over the years you plan to stay.

**Do I need a whole-house filter or just reverse osmosis in Miami?**
Reverse osmosis treats drinking water at one tap. A whole-house system treats showers, laundry and every faucet, including chloramine and hardness. Many Miami homes use both.

**How long does a whole-house install take?**
At Biscayne Water, 4 to 6 hours including reverse osmosis, with the water off for only 30 to 45 minutes.
