## What Miami water does to a house

Miami-Dade's 2025 water report shows chloramine averaging 2.5 ppm, with readings up to the 4.0 ppm maximum. That is the pool smell in your shower and the taste in your coffee. Carbon filtration also lowers the disinfection by-products the report lists: trihalomethanes averaged 50 ppb against an 80 ppb limit.

Then there is the scale. The white crust on your shower glass and faucet heads is calcium and magnesium from the limestone aquifer. A softener stops it at the main line.

<small>Source: Miami-Dade Water and Sewer Department, 2025 Water Quality Report, Main System. [Full breakdown](/water-quality/miami-dade-wasd/).</small>
