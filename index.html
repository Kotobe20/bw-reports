Biscayne Water — Nov 12 Sales & Funnel Playbook

Prepared Nov 12, 2026 · Close-Rate Deep Dive + Zoom Funnel Bottlenecks + Josh Hamilton VSL Playbook

1. Doug Alexander Close-Rate Deep Dive (TL;DR)

Bottom line: Close rate per lead is not broken. The real problems are a 72% drop in fresh leads and reps not asking for the sale.

Green Flags (What's Working)

  1. Average ticket jumped: $4,626 in Nov (up from $3,569 in Oct) and $4,949 this week, with 0 cancels so far in Nov.
  2. Kept close rate per lead held steady: 8.5% week of Oct 5 vs. 12.1% last week.
  3. Fresh leads (<1 day old) answer 22% of dials (vs. 8% on 14+ day leads).
  4. Front half of calls improved: Walking the water report rose 47% to 56%; showing the menu jumped 24% to 60%.
  5. Speed-to-lead improved: Median dropped from 4.0 min (Oct) to 2.1 min (Nov). Under 5 min closes ~17–19% vs. ~10% at 5m–2h.
  6. Spanish Meta leads crush it: 12.5% close rate at $537 ad spend per close.

Red Flags (What's Hurting)

  1. Fresh leads dropped 72%: 330/week (Oct 5) down to 91 last week (58 Mon–Thu this week). Reps went from 12 fresh leads/day to 2.4.
  2. 57% of dials go to 14+ day old leads (8% pickup rate).
  3. Asking for the sale collapsed: 36% (Oct) → 33% (last wk) → 17% (this wk). 31 of 38 priced calls this week had zero ask.
  4. 72% of sales calls end with no next step (no date, no money). 1–10 confidence check was used on 0% of calls.
  5. Meta English CPL doubled to $125: $4,012 spent for 32 leads = 1 close (2.2%).
  6. Spanish leads wait 10 min median (vs. 1 min for English), slowing down the highest-ROI ad bucket.
  7. Money/price objections doubled from 19% to 36%.

Top 3 Actions Right Now

  1. Restore fresh lead flow (especially Spanish + Google/Organic) — every 100 fresh leads yields 8–12 kept closes.
  2. Run a 10-min/day back-half drill: 3 asks + 2 install dates + 1–10 check before hanging up. Asking 3+ times wins 65% vs. 15%; offering 2 dates wins 64% vs. 24%.
  3. Dial fresh leads first and hit 5–7 PM (15–16% pickup vs. 9–12% midday).

2. How the Zoom Funnel Fixes These Bottlenecks

  1. Fixes the "no ask / weak close" bottleneck: On phone calls, 31 of 38 priced calls had zero ask. Zoom forces a structured menu, visual price anchor, and a clear deposit/checkout step on screen.
  2. Kills the #1 loss reason ("need to talk to my spouse"): Spouse stall killed 54 deals in Oct alone. Zoom lets you book both decision-makers onto one 20-minute call and show the water report to both at once.
  3. Fixes the 36% price objection jump: Pitching $4,600–$9,000 blind over the phone triggers sticker shock. Screen-sharing the water report, contaminant visuals, and financing side-by-side builds value before the price drops.
  4. Stretches fresh lead ROI at $125 CPL: Pre-framed Zoom appointments filter out tire-kickers and raise show-to-close when reps only get 2.4 fresh leads/day.

2 things Zoom won't fix on its own: (1) Speed-to-lead still has to be under 5 min to book them onto Zoom (especially Spanish leads waiting 10 min), and (2) reps still must run the 3-ask + 2-date close on Zoom.

3. Josh Hamilton (@joshhamiltonsworld) VSL & Funnel Playbook

Josh Hamilton (Partner / CRO at So Shall We) runs the Grid + Triangle framework and VSL Stacking across 300+ VSLs, including home-service brands like Rhino Shield Florida and Can Do Crew.

Use Format #3: Vlog VSL (Not a Studio Talking Head)

  • "If the place sells it, film the place." Film real South Florida homes, water tests, staining, and installs.
  • Talk like you're at a bar with a neighbor. Homeowners smell fake hype.
  • Turn on chapters so buyers can jump straight to price, proof, and FAQs.

The 4-Box Grid (Steal This Exact Script)

  1. Rapid Capture (first 60 sec): Open on the #1 objection, give the whole offer in 45 sec, and show the agenda.
  2. Road Map: What they get, the upside, the pain avoided, why you, and how it works (pulled word-for-word from your best sales call).
  3. Disqualify + Price (first 90 sec): Spend way longer on who it is not for (renters, pitcher buyers). State the price flat ($3,500–$9,000) — don't hide it until Zoom.
  4. Remove Doubt: Smash 12 months of real call objections + show anti-case studies (what untreated South Florida water ruins + jobs you turned down).

VSL Stacking (+23% Show Rate, 33% Shorter Sales Cycle)

Don't rely on one VSL. Put a short video at every drop-off point:

  1. Main VSL → books the Zoom.
  2. Didn't book? Auto-text a short objection clip (cost / spouse / "already have a softener").
  3. Booked Zoom? Send to a Confirmation Page Stack: a 5-question FAQ video from real calls (drives most of the +23% lift), full price/scope doc, anti-case studies, and a pre-call homework quiz (zip, city vs. well, photos).
  4. Day before Zoom: Send a 2-min "movie trailer" selfie video (proof + what to have ready).
  5. On the Zoom: They are already pre-sold. Spend the call on their house, not pitching Biscayne.

Home-Service Proof (So Shall We Agency)

  • Rhino Shield Florida: Localized Tampa/Orlando/Jax/Gainesville ads → cost/appt dropped ~$1,300 → $840, ROAS 3.4x → 6.1x, 2x bookings in under 4 months.
  • Can Do Crew (HVAC/Plumbing): Real tech/install videos + SMS/email follow-up + maintenance plan → cut CPL 50%, grew $3.1M → $5.8M in 12 months.
