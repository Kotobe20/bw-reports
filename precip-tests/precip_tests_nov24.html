TL;DR

  • This list is for dealer “precip” (precipitation) demo kits used at the kitchen sink to show hardness minerals falling out, plus soap, iron, pH, and a mini softener. It is not the Nov 12 mail-in / screening kit list (ITS, Hanna, Tap Score, AEL).
  • Best starter buy for Biscayne: WET Platinum kit ($663.05) + WET chloramine meter kit ($142.23) +, for wells, WET hydrogen sulfide ($56.74) and tannin ($46.82). That bag is about $908.84 before tax and shipping.
  • South Florida city water is chloraminated. The chlorine check inside most demo kits is a simple OTO / free-chlorine test. That can miss or understate chloramine. Add a real chloramine / total-chlorine test before you talk disinfectant with a Miami-Dade or Broward homeowner.
  • Tell the truth about the precip test. It shows hardness minerals, not pollution or a health hazard. Florida law and the Water Quality Association both flag this.

Legal caution

Florida Statute § 817.558 makes it a first-degree misdemeanor to make false or misleading claims about tap-water contamination when selling a water-treatment device. The Florida Attorney General warns that in-home “color change / particles form” demos are often sold as proof the water is polluted, and the Water Quality Association says a precipitation test (chemical or electric) measures hardness, not pollution or a health concern, and must not be described that way. Sources: Fla. Stat. § 817.558 (2026); Florida Attorney General, water-treatment devices; WQA, Jan 19, 2022 (includes a Titusville, FL incident).

How this differs from the earlier kit list

Around Nov 12 we posted a general water-test options list (group msg 79668). That work was about screening kits and certified labs: ITS Water Quality kit $46.99, Hanna DiST 1 $79.99, Hach IR-20 iron/manganese, Tap Score, AEL Miramar, and so on. Those are still the right tools if you want a number, a mail-in PFAS/metals panel, or a Florida-certified report.

They are not what water-treatment reps usually mean by a “precip test.” A precip kit is a visual sales demo: add reagent, minerals fall out, then show the same water after the mini softener, plus a soap comparison. Use both. Do not treat a precip bottle as a lab result, and do not use a lab kit as your only in-home visual.
