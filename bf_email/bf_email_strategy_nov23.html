Why Instantly is the wrong fit: it's made for sending to strangers by rotating many warmed mailboxes. This list is people who already asked Biscayne Water for help or bought from us. That's normal marketing email, and the main things that matter are proper domain authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), a working one-click unsubscribe, and low spam complaints. Gmail requires spam rates under 0.3% for all senders, plus one-click unsubscribe and DMARC once you send 5,000+/day.

Close's role (opinion): keep Close for follow-up. Reps call or email the people who click the quiz link. Don't do the blast from it.

Setup steps (in order)

  1. DNS (Cloudflare, ~10 min): add the 3 records Resend gave us: DKIM TXT resend._domainkey.send, MX send.send → feedback-smtp.us-east-1.amazonses.com (priority 10), SPF TXT send.send = v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all. Then click Verify in Resend. The root domain already has DMARC (p=none).
  2. Resend plan: upgrade to Pro Marketing $40/mo (AG's OK still pending).
  3. List scrub: pull a fresh Close export with first name, unsubscribed, bounced and do-not-contact status and drop anyone opted out. Today's file has only email + source, no names or status. Spot-check for typos.
  4. Import + segments in Resend: 2 segments. Customers (HCP-tagged, ~296) get the customers version. Leads (the rest, ~2,220) get the leads version.
  5. Fill placeholders: [END DATE], [BUSINESS ADDRESS] (HCP shows 1600 Ponce de Leon Blvd, 10th Fl, Coral Gables, FL 33134), [FINANCING DISCLAIMER]. Confirm the HSA line.
  6. Test: send to AG, Steve and Doug (Gmail + iPhone). Check links, unsubscribe, and the spam folder. Turn on click tracking.
