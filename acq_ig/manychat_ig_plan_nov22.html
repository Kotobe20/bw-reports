Instagram → sales with ManyChat: how we do Hormozi’s Weekly Training #2, fast

Steve asked: “how do we implement this asap.” Short answer: about one day of work. ManyChat sends a DM when someone comments a word, replies to a story, or follows us. The DM sends them to our quiz. The quiz already puts leads in Close.

TL;DR: the 5 steps

  1. Pay for ManyChat Pro: $39/mo, or $29/mo billed yearly. It has a 14-day free trial. AG approves.
  2. Connect @biscaynewater: switch it to a Professional account, turn on “Allow access to messages,” and give ManyChat every Meta permission. AG does the login and 2FA.
  3. Turn off any other bot on IG DMs, including Valentina/GoHighLevel and Meta auto-replies, so customers don’t get two replies. AG checks.
  4. Grok builds 4 automations: comment WATER, comment AGUA, story reply, and new follower. Each sends a 3-message DM (now, about +22h, +48h) with a tracked quiz link.
  5. Post the trigger: put “comment WATER” in the first seconds of the video and at the top of the caption. Repost our top reels with the keyword, put a story CTA up 1–2 times a week, and make the bio link point to the quiz.

Labels: Fact = checked in a source this turn. Assumption = my best read, not checked. Lesson timestamps come from an auto-transcript (faster-whisper), so a few words may be off.

3 things to know first. (1) Our quiz is not a “water report for your ZIP.” It is a 60-second quiz that checks your ZIP, asks what worries you, and sends the lead to Close. So the DMs call it a quiz. They don’t promise a report. A real per-city report is a v2 add-on (section 2.6). (2) The quiz is English only, so Spanish DMs link to an English quiz until a Spanish one exists. (3) The quiz page shows system prices, and the DMs never do. That is a site choice, not an ad. I didn’t change it.

What the community added

  • Where the new-follower trigger lives: it’s a setting, not a normal automation. Go to Settings → Channel → “Say hi to new followers.” (Kevin Gong and Stephanie Lombardo, comments, Mar 17–18) Some members said it errored or wasn’t offered to them (KhaazRa MaaRanu, Theodore Harvey, Brandee Gaar). ManyChat support’s fix: sign out of FB/IG and reconnect with “Use another profile” (Brandee Gaar, Apr 19).
  • Stephanie Lombardo, “My Lessons from 3yrs in ManyChat” (link): use 2–3 keywords at most and reuse them on every post. Don’t build a new lead magnet for each post. Repost your top viral videos with a keyword. Keep a template to copy. Ungated Google Docs work great. Put the CTA as an overlay plus in the caption, or the caption alone. (Our saved copy of her post cuts off at “Using Your Skool Community as a Lead Magnet,” so I haven’t read that part.)
  • Hormozi’s Marketing Implementation checklist (kb/11_files/lesson_gdoc_1PYpG0…): Instagram should have 3 CTA spots: a bio link with one destination and no Linktree, a ManyChat keyword on Reels, and a story CTA 1–2× a week.
  • Europe/Japan: Meta blocks new-follower auto-DMs to people located in the EU (Jan Seiler, Mar 20). That doesn’t affect South Florida.

2.2 Connect Instagram (AG on his phone, about 10 minutes)

  1. IG app → Settings and activity → Messages and story replies → Message controls → Connected tools → turn on Allow access to messages. (help.manychat.com “Can’t connect Instagram to Manychat”)
  2. In ManyChat, add the Instagram channel, log in as @biscaynewater, do the 2FA, and keep every permission box checked. That’s Alex’s “give it all the permissions” [05:50–05:55].
  3. If it loops: in ManyChat, go to Help → Refresh permissions. Also check that Meta Business Integrations → ManyChat has every toggle on. (same ManyChat article)
  4. In ManyChat, go to Settings → Channel → “Say hi to new followers” to turn on the follower DM.

Two bots can’t both answer our IG DMs. The old Valentina bot is on GoHighLevel. Its wallet is suspended, it has a Messenger node but no IG node, and it has no DM triggers (my Nov 20 read). Biscayne Water stopped using GoHighLevel on Nov 13. Assumption: GoHighLevel may still be connected to the IG/FB page inside Meta. Before launch, AG should (a) remove GoHighLevel/LeadConnector under Meta Business Integrations, or confirm it isn’t there, and (b) turn off any Meta Business Suite auto-replies or FAQs on IG. Otherwise a person who comments “WATER” could get two DMs. Valentina’s rules still help: keep the DMs short, check the area first and send the quiz link early. ManyChat replaces her on IG.

2.3 Triggers (4 automations, all built from one template)

TriggerSetup
Comment WATER (English)Set to fire on “any post or reel.” Lombardo’s advice is to reuse one word, so we don’t make a new one per post. Accept “water” in any case, plus “Water 💧.” A public reply goes under the comment, like “sent it to your DMs 💧.”
Comment AGUA (Spanish)Same as above, but it sends the Spanish DMs.
Story replyFires when someone replies to a story with WATER or AGUA. Post a story CTA 1–2× a week, per Hormozi’s checklist.
New followerA soft hello that asks “want the quiz?” Only send the link after they say yes (Alex [11:46–12:02]; Lombardo).

Assumption, verify in ManyChat: Meta only lets automated DMs go out within 24 hours of the person’s last action. Tapping a DM button counts as an action. So the nudge goes at about +22h, not +24h, and the +48h message only goes to people who tapped or replied, so it can still get through. ManyChat shows a warning in the builder if a step is outside the window. Also, test with a different IG account, because IG won’t fire automations on your own comments (ManyChat community answer).

2.6 Lead magnet: honest now, better in v2

  • Day 1: the quiz itself, which is Alex’s option A, “give it straight.” It works now and already sends leads to Close.
  • v2 (about 3–4 hrs of Grok work): a free ungated Google Doc for each city, “Your city’s water report, explained.” Lombardo says ungated docs work great. Each doc links the city’s real yearly water quality report, gives EWG numbers with fine print, and has the quiz link inside (Alex: links to the next thing [13:11–13:17]). EWG fine print on every number: “EWG Tap Water Database, [utility] (PWS [ID]), checked [date]. EWG health guidelines are stricter than federal limits; [utility]’s water meets federal limits. Your home may differ.” So far we have EWG data only for Miami-Dade Water and Sewer (FL4130871). Broward cities still need it.

  4. Test-strip before and after at a real customer’s home, with real readings only. This is the BF remake idea. Ogilvy: an honest demo, “don’t cheat.”
  5. “What’s in Miami-Dade tap water?” carousel with EWG numbers and fine print, plus “comment WATER.” Broward waits for its data.

  7. Story CTA 1–2× a week, like “reply WATER and we’ll send the quiz.”
